Privacy policy

Last updated: 11/07/26

Muse Tools is used only by its owner, for the owner's own Google and YouTube accounts.

Data the app reads

  • YouTube: channel and video statistics and YouTube Analytics reports of the owner's channels, with read-only scopes (youtube.readonly, yt-analytics.readonly).
  • Google Drive: the owner's own files that the owner uploads and downloads.

How the data is used and kept

  • The data is used only to plan and improve the owner's own videos.
  • Sign-in tokens and reports are kept on the owner's computer only.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

The use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoke access

Remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
